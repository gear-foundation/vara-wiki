# Welcome to Vara.eth

Vara.eth brings **high-performance computation** directly to Ethereum. Build apps that were previously impossible on-chain — with parallel execution, 2 GB memory per program, and users who never pay gas.

<Callout type="note">
  **Vara.eth is proven live on Ethereum — start building today.** The TestNet and MainNet campaigns ran the full stack end to end. [One of Us](https://github.com/gear-foundation/one-of-us) drew 931 members — one click each, no gas paid by users; [Robo Miner](https://github.com/gear-foundation/robo-miner) put AI agents into a live on-chain arena. Both are open source and work as reference applications.

  Those campaigns are complete, and the Gear-operated public endpoints (Ethereum RPC, Beacon, and the Vara.eth validator network) are no longer served. Everything else is unchanged — the Router and wVARA contracts stay deployed and verifiable on Ethereum Mainnet and Hoodi, and the whole stack is open source. Point `ethexe` at any Ethereum RPC provider, or [run your own node](/docs/vara-eth/setup/local-node), and reproduce the entire flow. You can also build with [Agent Skills](/docs/vara-eth/setup/agent-skills).
</Callout>
