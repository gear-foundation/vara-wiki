# Networks

Vara.eth runs on Ethereum. The testnet is deployed on **Ethereum Hoodi**, and mainnet on **Ethereum Mainnet**.

<Callout type="note" title="Endpoint Availability">
  The `gear-tech.io` RPC endpoints below were operated by Gear for the launch campaigns and are no longer served. Chain IDs, explorers and deployed contracts are unchanged — substitute any Ethereum RPC provider for Hoodi or Mainnet, and [run your own node](/docs/vara-eth/setup/local-node) for the Vara.eth side.
</Callout>

## Testnet (Ethereum Hoodi)

The Vara.eth testnet deployment on Ethereum Hoodi:

- Chain ID: `560048`
- Ethereum RPC: any Hoodi provider (the retired Gear endpoint was `https://hoodi-reth-rpc.gear-tech.io`)
- Explorer: [hoodi.etherscan.io](https://hoodi.etherscan.io)

For the full endpoint matrix (HTTPS, WebSocket, health checks, and connection examples), use:

→ [Network Endpoints](/docs/vara-eth/reference/network-endpoints)

For Router and related contract addresses, use:

→ [Contract Addresses](/docs/vara-eth/reference/contract-addresses)

## Mainnet (Ethereum Mainnet)

The Vara.eth mainnet deployment on Ethereum Mainnet:

- Chain ID: `1`
- Ethereum RPC: any Ethereum Mainnet provider (the retired Gear endpoint was `https://mainnet-reth-rpc.gear-tech.io`)
- Explorer: [etherscan.io](https://etherscan.io)

For the full endpoint matrix (HTTPS, WebSocket, Beacon, Validator RPCs), use:

→ [Network Endpoints](/docs/vara-eth/reference/network-endpoints)

For Router and wVARA contract addresses, use:

→ [Contract Addresses](/docs/vara-eth/reference/contract-addresses)
