The official bridge interface is available at [**bridge.vara.network**](https://bridge.vara.network/). It supports transfers of the following assets between Vara and Ethereum:

- **VARA (Vara Native) ↔ wVARA** (on Ethereum)
- **ETH (Ethereum Native) ↔ wETH** (on Vara)
- **USDT (on Ethereum) ↔ wUSDT** (on Vara)
- **USDC (on Ethereum) ↔ wUSDC** (on Vara)
- **wBTC (on Ethereum) ↔ wBTC** (on Vara)

<Callout type="warn" title="Check the token address">
    The bridge works only with the token contracts listed in the [Developer Hub](/docs/vara-network/bridge/developer_hub). The interface shows the contract address next to each asset — compare it before you confirm a transfer.
</Callout>

## Quick Guide: Bridging Assets

### Wallet Requirements

**From Vara → Ethereum** Connect your Vara wallet by selecting one of [the supported wallets](https://vara.network/ecosystem?tags=6382654c-162d-4ed7-9215-b9504b60e72c).

**From Ethereum → Vara** Connect your Ethereum-compatible wallet (e.g., [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/), [Trust Wallet](https://trustwallet.com/) etc)

---

### Step 1: Select Asset & Amount

- Pick the direction and the asset you want to transfer (**VARA**, **ETH**, **USDT**, **USDC** or **wBTC**).
- Specify the **exact amount** you want to bridge.

---

### Step 2: Enter Destination Address

- Provide the **receiving wallet address** on the **target network**.
- Use a wallet you control. Deposit addresses of exchanges may not credit transfers that arrive from a bridge contract.

---

### Step 3: Choose the Claim Type

- **Automatic** (recommended) — you pay the fee shown in the interface, and a relayer delivers the assets directly to the destination wallet.
- **Manual claim** — you complete the delivery on the target network yourself and pay its network fee.

The expected fee is shown before you confirm.

---

### Step 4: Confirm and Initiate the Transfer

- If your wallet is not already connected, click **Connect Wallet**
- Click **Transfer** to start the process
- Review the **transaction details**
- Approve and **sign the transaction**

---

### Step 5: Track Your Transaction

The **Transactions** tab lets you check the status of current and past transfers without leaving the interface.

A transfer is not instant. It has to wait for finality on the source network and for the bridge to accept the proof on the target network, so it usually takes from **tens of minutes to a few hours**. Do not send the same transfer again while it is in progress.

<Callout type="idea" title="Who Pays the Transfer Fees?">
    The gas token required always depends on the **source network**:

    *   **Vara → Ethereum**: You only need **VARA** tokens. *(No ETH is required for the initial transfer.)*
    *   **Ethereum → Vara**: You need **ETH**, **regardless** of the asset you are bridging (VARA, ETH, or stablecoins).

    *Tip: Always keep a small amount of the native gas token in your source wallet to avoid transaction failures.*
</Callout>
