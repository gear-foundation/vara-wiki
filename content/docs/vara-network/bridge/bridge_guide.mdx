Use the official [bridge interface](https://bridge.vara.network/) to check which assets and directions currently accept new transfers. Availability for new transfers is separate from the ability to claim an existing transfer. The [Developer Hub](/docs/vara-network/bridge/developer_hub) lists deployed contracts, not live route status.

| On Vara | On Ethereum |
| --- | --- |
| VARA | BEEFY bridge WVARA |
| WETH | ETH (the bridge uses WETH on-chain) |
| WUSDT | USDT |
| WUSDC | USDC |
| WBTC | WBTC |

These are the bridge's asset pairs, not a list of open routes. Check the interface before initiating a transfer.

<Callout type="warn" title="Check the token address">
    Match the chain and token contract to the [Developer Hub](/docs/vara-network/bridge/developer_hub). In particular, BEEFY bridge WVARA and the Vara.eth Router's wVARA are different ERC-20 tokens with the same ticker.
</Callout>

## Quick Guide: Bridging Assets

### Wallet Requirements

**From Vara → Ethereum** Connect your Vara wallet by selecting one of [the supported wallets](https://vara.network/ecosystem?tags=6382654c-162d-4ed7-9215-b9504b60e72c).

**From Ethereum → Vara** Connect your Ethereum-compatible wallet (e.g., [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/), [Trust Wallet](https://trustwallet.com/) etc)

---

### Step 1: Select Asset & Amount

- Select an available direction and asset in the bridge interface.
- Specify the **exact amount** you want to bridge.

---

### Step 2: Enter Destination Address

- Provide the **receiving wallet address** on the **target network**.
- Use a wallet you control. Deposit addresses of exchanges may not credit transfers that arrive from a bridge contract.

---

### Step 3: Choose the Claim Type

- **Automatic delivery**, where offered — pay the quoted relay fee to have a relayer submit delivery. Paying the fee is not confirmation of delivery.
- **Manual claim**, where offered — submit the claim on the destination network and pay its gas fee. Open the original transfer in **Transactions** and use its claim action once available. If no claim action is available, keep the original transaction hash; do not start a replacement transfer.

---

### Step 4: Confirm and Initiate the Transfer

- If your wallet is not already connected, click **Connect Wallet**
- Click **Transfer** to start the process
- Review the **transaction details**
- Approve and **sign the transaction**

---

### Step 5: Track Your Transaction

Use **Transactions** to track the original transfer. Allow **tens of minutes to a few hours** as a planning estimate, not a deadline. Delivery waits for source finality, proof availability, and destination execution; Vara → Ethereum also waits for the queue root to mature. Pauses or proof delays can take longer. Check that the intended asset arrived in the destination wallet. If you expected native VARA, check the native balance and any pending claim, not just a wrapped-token credit.

For transfers initiated before the Ethereum contract cutover, retain the original source transaction hash and reconcile it against the original deployment. Do not resend the transfer or switch contract addresses to try to claim it.

<Callout type="idea" title="Who Pays the Transfer Fees?">
    * **Source gas:** Pay in VARA when sending from Vara, or ETH when sending from Ethereum.
    * **Outbound transport fee:** Vara charges a separate protocol fee to queue the outbound message. It still applies when you intend to self-relay.
    * **Delivery:** Pay a separate relay fee for automatic delivery where offered, or pay gas in the destination chain's native token when claiming yourself (ETH on Ethereum, VARA on Vara).
</Callout>
