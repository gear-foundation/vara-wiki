## Vara Bridge Deployments

<Callout type="info" title="Mainnet only">
    These are mainnet deployments. The former Vara bridge testnet is no longer available. A listed contract is not a guarantee that a transfer or claim is currently available; check the [Bridge Guide](/docs/vara-network/bridge/bridge_guide) for how to check your route. Develop and test before using mainnet funds.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Ethereum contracts were redeployed">
    The Ethereum side of the bridge now verifies Vara finality with a BEEFY light client. The contracts were redeployed for this change, including **MessageQueue**, **ERC20Manager**, **Bridging Payment**, **WVARA** and the governance contracts.

    Use the addresses below for integrations with the BEEFY deployment. They do not migrate balances, allowances, or in-flight transfers. For a transfer initiated before the cutover, retain its original transaction and use the contracts that handled it to identify the claim or recovery path. Do not resend or substitute the new addresses.
</Callout>

The Ethereum light client on Vara (`checkpoint-light-client`) is not listed here because its address changes whenever the client is replaced. Read the current address on-chain: call `EndpointFor(slot)` on **historical-proxy** to get the active event-verification program, then call `CheckpointLightClientAddress` on that program.

The outbound message queue lives in the `gear_eth_bridge` pallet. Programs reach it through the [Bridge Built-in Actor](/docs/developing/build/builtinactors/bia-bridge).
